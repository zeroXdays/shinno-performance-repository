Description:
Control the Shinno RGB kits on your Infiniti Q50 or G37 from your iPhone — emblem, mirrors, and bumper.

Turn the kits on, allow Bluetooth, and add each lighting device in Scan. Assign the zone, then set color, brightness, and modes. Speed goes from 0 (slow) to 100 (fast).

You can save more than one car. Switch between a Q50 and a G37; each keeps its own devices. G37 is emblem-only for now. Q60 and Q70 show up as “soon” and aren’t selectable yet.

No account. No in-app purchases. Your garage stays on your phone.
