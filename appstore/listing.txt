Subtitle (30):
RGB controller for Q50/G37

Category:

Description:
Shinno Sync is the iPhone controller for Shinno RGB kits on Infiniti Q50 and G37 — emblem, mirrors, and bumper.

Power the lighting devices that shipped with your kits, allow Bluetooth, and the app scans for them. Add each device, assign a zone, then set color, brightness, power, and lighting modes. Speed 0 is slow, 100 is fast.

Save both a Q50 and a G37 in the garage and switch between them — each car keeps its own kits. G37 is emblem-only in this version. Q60 and Q70 are listed as soon and cannot be selected yet. Grille badge control is not in this version.

There is no account, no in-app shop, and no demo mode. Names, colors, and paired devices stay on this iPhone.
