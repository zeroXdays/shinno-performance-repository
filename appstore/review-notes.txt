Shinno Sync (com.shinnoperformance.lights) is our iPhone app for controlling Shinno RGB kits on Infiniti Q50 and G37 — emblem, mirrors, and bumper. Customers buy the hardware from us at shinnoperformance.com; this app is how they set color, brightness, power, and modes over Bluetooth.

Who it’s for: people who already own a Shinno kit. There’s no account, no login, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no user-generated content. Everything about the garage stays on the phone.

Important for review: this only works with real powered kits nearby. We don’t ship a demo or simulator mode on purpose — without the lights, Scan is empty and Color/Modes won’t show anything useful. If you need hardware, email support@shinnoperformance.com and we’ll send a sample. Please include a shipping address and your case ID.

How to use it on a review phone:
1. Turn Bluetooth on and open the app.
2. Pick Q50 or G37 at the top (Q60/Q70 say “soon” and can’t be selected yet; grille isn’t in this build).
3. Go to Scan, power the Shinno products near the phone, tap Rescan if needed, and add each device.
4. On Devices, assign each one to Emblem, Mirrors, or Bumper.
5. Color tab: tap a zone, turn power on, pick a color / brightness.
6. Modes tab: pick a mode and use Speed (0 is slow, 100 is fast). Paths show on Q50 when zones are paired; they’re hidden on G37 for now.
7. Settings is just privacy, agreement, and how-to — no sign-in.

Devices usually show up as ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB, MELK, or LEDBLE. Keep them powered and within a few meters. G37 is emblem-only in this version.

We can also send a screen recording of the full flow with kits running if that helps. Same contact: support@shinnoperformance.com.

Other details you asked about:
- No login credentials — there isn’t an account system.
- No region-specific features; same app everywhere.
- Networking: Bluetooth for the lights. Optional check against https://shinnoperformance.com/api/verify-purchase only if a license check runs; lighting control itself doesn’t need the internet. No analytics, ads, auth SDKs, or AI services.
- Not a regulated-industry app. Hardware and branding are ours (Shin No Performance LLC).
